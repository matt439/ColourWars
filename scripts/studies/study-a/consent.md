# Study A: consent

Print this sheet, or read it aloud and record the answer in the session log's
`consent` and `consent_date` columns (`verbal` and the date).
Keep signed sheets apart from the drawings and recordings.

## What the study is

ColourWars is a game drawn to look as if a person made it in Windows 98 Paint.
We want to learn which small drawing mistakes people really make with a mouse,
so the game's art can include believable ones. You will draw two or three
simple pictures in a web copy of Paint, three times each. It takes about 35
minutes.

## What we record

- Your finished drawings, as image files.
- A recording of **the screen only**: no camera and no microphone.
- Notes on which tools you used and how often you pressed undo.
- Which hand you use the mouse with, and whether you have used a pixel-art or
  paint program before.

We do not record your name with any of this. Your drawings and notes carry only
an ID such as `p03`. The list that links your name to your ID is kept apart
from the data and is destroyed when the study ends.

## How it is used

- Two people count the mistakes in each drawing, and a script measures some of
  them.
- Only **summaries** are used in the game: for example "a line overshoots its
  corner by one square in a third of drawings". **Your drawings are never put
  into the game, its source code or its repository**, and are never published.
- Drawings and recordings are stored on the experimenter's computer, outside
  the game's files, and deleted by [date, set before the first session].

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask for your data to be deleted until [date, set before the first
  session]. After that only the anonymous counts remain.
- You can ask questions now or later: [experimenter contact].
