# Study A: what you will do

Thank you for taking part. You will draw two or three small pictures in a copy
of an old paint program. It is not a test of skill. We want to see the ordinary
small mistakes everyone makes with a mouse, so please draw at your normal pace
and do not try to be perfect.

## The program

- The program is [jspaint.app](https://jspaint.app), a copy of Windows 98
  Paint, in its Classic theme. The experimenter sets it up for you.
- Use any tool you like: pencil, brush, line, rectangle, ellipse, fill bucket,
  select, copy and paste.
- **You can only undo your last 3 actions.** Paint 98 kept only three steps.
  Once you have undone them, older steps are gone: after you draw again, you
  can undo only what you have done since, up to 3 actions. If you need to fix
  something older, draw over it.
- Do not open other programs or files.

## Time

Each picture has **5 minutes**. The experimenter tells you when 1 minute is
left and when to stop. An unfinished picture is fine; stop when told.

## The canvas

The experimenter sets the canvas size before each picture. There are two kinds.

- **Z (zoomed):** a small canvas shown at 400% with the grid on, so every
  square is one dot. For the person it is 17 squares wide and 30 tall.
- **F (full size):** a larger canvas at 100%, with no grid. For the person it is
  136 by 240 dots.

The other pictures use these canvases:

| Picture | Z canvas | F canvas |
|---|---|---|
| Person | 17 x 30 at 400%, grid on | 136 x 240 at 100% |
| Bricks | 49 x 13 at 400%, grid on | 392 x 104 at 100% |
| Brush pot | 64 x 80 at 400%, grid on | 256 x 320 at 100% |

## The three tasks

You do each picture three times, in this order.

1. **D, from a description.** You read the description below and draw it.
   You do not see the picture.
2. **C, copy.** The picture opens beside the canvas. Copy it as closely as you
   can.
3. **T, trace.** A pale grey copy of the picture is already on the canvas. Draw
   over it. You do not need to clean up any pale grey you miss.

Use black for outlines and the greys in the palette for coloured areas, as
the picture does.

## The descriptions (task D)

Read only the description for the picture the experimenter names.

**Person.** A small person standing still and facing you, drawn with black
outlines. They wear a grey cap whose peak sticks out to the left only. The head
is a white box, a little wider than it is tall, with a small ear on each side,
two dot eyes and a small lopsided smile. A short neck joins the head to a
box-shaped body. The body wears grey overalls with two straps over a white
shirt. Short grey sleeves end in white hands at each side. Two thin straight
grey legs end in black feet. The whole person is about twice as tall as it is
wide.

**Bricks.** A short wall of grey bricks, three rows high and six bricks long,
with a black outline round the whole wall and black lines between the bricks.
Each brick is twice as long as it is tall. The middle row is moved along by half
a brick, so it has a half brick at each end.

**Brush pot.** A pot holding three paintbrushes, drawn with black outlines. The
pot is a grey bucket shape, a little narrower at the bottom, with rounded lower
corners and an oval rim at the top seen slightly from above. Three brushes stand
in it: one straight up in the middle, one leaning left and one leaning right.
Each has a long light-grey handle and a wider, flat grey head at the top.
