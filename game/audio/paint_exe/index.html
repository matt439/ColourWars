Paint.exe audio review

Recorded paint, paper and mechanisms. Original arrangements for ukulele, upright piano and hand percussion.

Production candidates. Listening approval and in-game mix acceptance are pending. These files do not change the game's live audio.

Production notes · Every source and licence · Exact audition timeline

Hear the set together

Same 49-second scripted scene at 1, 2 and 4 players, using the same output gain. Intro room → countdown → movement and weapons → time-up → reveal. These are offline arrangements, not gameplay recordings. Useful cues should stay readable as players are added.
