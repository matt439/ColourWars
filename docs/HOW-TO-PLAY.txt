ColourWars - how to play
========================

A split-screen paint-shooter for up to four players. Two teams, one arena;
the team that has painted more of it when the clock runs out wins.


Starting the game
-----------------

Built from source? Open the executable path printed by build.cmd; there is
no ZIP to extract. For a downloaded ZIP:

1. Extract the whole ZIP first. In Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose
   "Extract All...". Running ColourWars.exe from inside the ZIP does not work:
   the game needs the files beside it.

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click ColourWars.exe. You can put
   that folder anywhere, for example Documents or the desktop. Nothing needs
   installing.

3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". The game is not signed with a
   publisher certificate, so Windows does not recognise it yet. Choose
   "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once for each copy.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card or chip that supports
Direct3D 11, which nearly every PC from the last ten years has. It does not
run over Remote Desktop or in most virtual machines.


Playing
-------

New here? On Home, move right from Play to How to play. Its three pages
explain practice/local setup, show your saved controls, and explain results
and rematches. Back returns to Home; the guide does not change your setup.

For a quick match against a Normal bot, choose Play a bot on Home. Review
the Sprayer and Grenade preset and choose Continue to Join. Confirm on Join
Players, then pick an arena. Choose Customise on the preset screen to use
the full setup with your own teams, tools, bot difficulty and match rules.

Play > Single Match (or Best of 3 / Best of 5) > choose how many players.
The device you choose with becomes Player 1.

On Join Players, everyone else presses A on their pad (Enter for keyboard
and mouse) to take a seat. Player 1 can move to an empty seat with Up/Down
and press Left/Right to add a bot and choose Easy, Normal or Hard. Once each
seat is filled, Player 1 presses A / Enter to continue. For a versus match,
choose teams before Weapon Select.

For solo practice, choose Play > Single Match > 1 Player (practice).
Confirm on Join Players with A / Enter, then choose a tool and a sub weapon
on Weapon Select. Choose Movement Course on Stage Select for lessons on
moving, jumping, dropping through platforms, refilling paint and using your
sub.

Up to four players: one may use the keyboard and mouse, the others need
Xbox-style (XInput) controllers. PlayStation and Switch controllers are not
recognised directly; they need software that presents an Xbox controller to
Windows, such as Steam Input.

                      Gamepad              Keyboard and mouse
  Move                Left stick           A / D
  Jump                A or left trigger    Space
  Aim                 Right stick          Mouse
  Shoot               Right trigger        Left mouse button
  Sub                 Right bumper (RB)    Right mouse button
  Drop through        Left stick down      S or Down
  Pause               Start                Escape

Rather not hold the trigger? Options > Controls / audio > Pad sticks has a
Toggle fire setting for each controller: turn it On and one press of Shoot
keeps firing until the next press.

The Umbrella primary fires five pellets in a short fan. Holding Shoot opens
the canopy on the side you aim toward. Enemy shots wear it down, teammates'
shots pass through, and players can walk around or through it. A broken
canopy takes three seconds to recover. You can keep firing during that time.

Sub is your sub weapon, carried beside your tool all match. Choose it on
Weapon Select: confirm your tool with A / Enter, pick the sub with Left/Right,
then press A / Enter again.

  Grenade             Lobbed along your aim. It bursts into a ring of paint
                      and hurts enemies caught in it.
  Sprinkler           Sticks where it lands and sprays paint around it for a
                      few seconds. Enemy shots break it.
  Paint wall          Stands beside you, on the side you aim, and stops enemy
                      shots for a few seconds. It drips paint on the floor
                      beneath it.
  Random              Picks one of the three for you.

A sub costs half a tank of paint and only fires when that much is in the
tank, and there is a short wait between uses. The small square beside PAINT
on your HUD is solid when it is ready. With Endless paint the cost goes and
only the wait remains.

Paint on the floor affects you too: your team's paint makes you move and
refill faster. Enemy paint slows you and stops your paint refilling; step
onto your own paint or unpainted ground to refill again.

On Sluice Works, paint the sides of the gate to open the lower passage for
your team. Either team can cross the bridge above it. For a different rule,
pick Turbulence Yard - Fill Trial in Stage Select. Paint the marked sides of
the centre block to fill its top, then defend your paint as the other team
tries to take it back.

Menus: left stick or D-pad / arrow keys to move, A / Enter (or a left
click) to choose, B / Escape (or a right click) to go back. A click acts on
the highlighted row, wherever the pointer is. Controls, volume, window size
and full screen are under Options. Options > Graphics offers CRT monitor
(restart), Off by default. Apply saves it for the next launch; restart to add
texture to the opening/result monitor glass. Reduce effects turns the texture
off. Gameplay stays clear. Back discards unapplied choices. To quit, choose
Exit on the main menu.

On Results, choose Save arena PNG to keep a picture of that round's finished
arena. The image goes in the "screens" subfolder of your save folder. Continue
goes straight to the rematch choices.


Settings, your own stages and removing the game
-----------------------------------------------

From Home, choose Options, move right from Graphics and select Open save
folder. This opens %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars in Explorer. Stages you build go
in the "stages" subfolder, which the game makes the first time it saves your
settings.

If your settings file, save_data.json, cannot be read or was written by a
newer version, the game keeps a copy in the same folder, named
save_data.unreadable-<n>.json or save_data.newer-<n>.json.

To update, extract the new ZIP and delete the old folder; your settings stay.
To remove the game, delete its folder, and %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars if you
also want your settings gone.

If the game stops with an error, the message box says why, and a short
report is written to %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars\last-error.log.
Copy the full error dialog (Ctrl+C while it is focused) when reporting it:
the short report does not contain that message. Include build-info.txt from
beside the executable and last-error.log if it was saved. If the game
closes with no message at all, last-error.log may still hold a short crash
report; include it.
