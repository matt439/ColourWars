ColourWars - how to play
========================

A split-screen paint-shooter for up to four players. Two teams, one arena;
the team that has painted more of it when the clock runs out wins.


Starting the game
-----------------

Built from source? Open the executable path printed by build.cmd; there is
no ZIP to extract. For a downloaded ZIP:

1. Extract the whole ZIP first. In Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose
   "Extract All...". Running ColourWars.exe from inside the ZIP does not work:
   the game needs the files beside it.

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click ColourWars.exe. You can put
   that folder anywhere, for example Documents or the desktop. Nothing needs
   installing.

3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". The game is not signed with a
   publisher certificate, so Windows does not recognise it yet. Choose
   "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once for each copy.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card or chip that supports
Direct3D 11, which nearly every PC from the last ten years has. It does not
run over Remote Desktop or in most virtual machines.


Playing
-------

Play > Single Match (or Best of 3 / Best of 5) > choose how many players.
The device you choose with becomes Player 1.

On Join Players, everyone else presses A on their pad (Enter for keyboard
and mouse) to take a seat. No friends around? Player 1 can move to an empty
seat with Up/Down and press Left/Right to turn it into a bot (Easy, Normal or
Hard). For solo practice, choose Single Match, then 1 player.
New? Choose Single Match > 1 Player > Movement Course for lesson cards on
moving, jumping, dropping through platforms and refilling your paint.

Up to four players: one may use the keyboard and mouse, the others need
Xbox-style (XInput) controllers. PlayStation and Switch controllers are not
recognised directly; they need software that presents an Xbox controller to
Windows, such as Steam Input.

                      Gamepad              Keyboard and mouse
  Move                Left stick           A / D
  Jump                A or left trigger    Space
  Aim                 Right stick          Mouse
  Shoot               Right trigger        Left mouse button
  Sub                 Right bumper (RB)    Right mouse button
  Drop through        Left stick down      S or Down
  Pause               Start                Escape

Sub is your sub weapon, carried beside your tool all match. Choose it on
Weapon Select: confirm your tool with A / Enter, pick the sub with Left/Right,
then press A / Enter again.

  Grenade             Lobbed along your aim. It bursts into a ring of paint
                      and hurts enemies caught in it.
  Sprinkler           Sticks where it lands and sprays paint around it for a
                      few seconds. Enemy shots break it.
  Paint wall          Stands beside you, on the side you aim, and stops enemy
                      shots for a few seconds. It drips paint on the floor
                      beneath it.
  Random              Picks one of the three for you.

A sub costs half a tank of paint and only fires when that much is in the
tank, and there is a short wait between uses. The small square beside PAINT
on your HUD is solid when it is ready. With Endless paint the cost goes and
only the wait remains.

Paint on the floor affects you too: your team's paint makes you move and
refill faster. Enemy paint slows you and stops your paint refilling; step
onto your own paint or unpainted ground to refill again.

Menus: left stick or D-pad / arrow keys to move, A / Enter to choose,
B / Escape to go back. Controls, volume, window size and full screen are
under Options. To quit, choose Exit on the main menu.


Settings, your own stages and removing the game
-----------------------------------------------

Settings are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars (paste that into Explorer's
address bar). Stages you build go in the "stages" folder there, which the
game makes the first time it saves your settings.

To update, extract the new ZIP and delete the old folder; your settings stay.
To remove the game, delete its folder, and %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars if you
also want your settings gone.

If the game stops with an error, the message box says why, and a short
report is written to %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColourWars\last-error.log.
Copy the full error dialog (Ctrl+C while it is focused) when reporting it:
the short report does not contain that message. Include build-info.txt from
beside the executable and last-error.log if it was saved.
